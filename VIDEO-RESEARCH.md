# Video research and verification — 2026-10-06

59 original videos, 118 concept placements. English-first with an optional Mandarin university lecture by Hung-yi Lee. Creator titles remain verbatim; viewing prompts, context and follow-up questions are bilingual.

Selections were researched through original YouTube pages/oEmbed metadata, educator descriptions, the StatQuest creator index, Harvard CS50, MIT OpenCourseWare, CrashCourse and university/DeepMind pages. Every catalog entry returned provider watch status OK and playableInEmbed=true on the checked date, and has a verified duration. This is provider permission/availability metadata, not proof of playback from every region.

Segments are full videos unless creator description chapter boundaries are recorded. Three Python chapter segments use those published boundaries; start/end bounds are checked against durations. Broad connections are explicitly labeled background; videos do not claim to reproduce each book-specific example. Creator corrections in the algorithm and Boolean-logic videos are noted in the guidance.

The BFS lecture played in the preview and remained playing through both language directions. The DFS detail player loaded after disclosure activation and unloaded on closing. Each of the59 selected players was then individually loaded through its lesson, its normal controls inspected and Play activated. Immediate media state was recorded in `data/video-player-checks.json`; those short checks mostly report time0 and cannot establish sustained playback. One ambiguous-frame inspection was retried successfully. Full-video playback, full transcript review and comprehensive regional tests were not performed. Original-source links remain usable when an embedded player is restricted.

YouTube integration follows [player parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters) and [embedded player identity](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality#embedded-player-api-client-identity): strict-origin-when-cross-origin, normal controls/branding, no autoplay, minimum200px player dimensions, source link, and no overlay over an active player.

No remote thumbnails, player requests or streams load before choosing Load video. Collapsed cards load nothing; closing an active detail unloads it. UI language changes captions/prompts without replacing the iframe. Audio language is listed separately; neither translated audio nor subtitles are promised. Streams are external and are not cached or rehosted.
